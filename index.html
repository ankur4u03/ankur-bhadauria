Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

Personal practice project | Sample Retail Sales Dataset

Practice project using sample data. I created this dashboard myself for learning. It is not a company assignment, and the figures are sample data, not real business results.
Project typePersonal practice project
DatasetSample Retail Sales Dataset
ToolsMicrosoft Power BI, Power Query, Data Visualization
Data size500 orders, 11 columns

Six KPIs

117MTotal SaleSum of Total_Sales
32.12MTotal ProfitSum of Profit
500Total OrdersCount of Order_ID
499Total CustomersCount of unique Customer_Name
233.50KAverage SaleAverage of Total_Sales
64.24KAverage ProfitAverage of Profit

Dashboard visualizations

  • Product Sales Analysis. Clustered column chart of total sales for each of the 6 products.
  • Monthly Sales Trend Analysis. Line (area) chart of total sales by month.
  • Region by Product table. Matrix of total sales for each region and product, with row and column totals.
  • Product Ranking. Funnel chart that ranks the 6 products by total sales.
  • Regional Sales Distribution. Donut chart of each region’s share of total sales.
  • Region slicer. Checkbox filter for East, North, South and West.

What the sample data shows

  • Total sales are 116,748,637 and total profit is about 32.12M.
  • Product sales are close: Printer is highest (about 20.38M) and Monitor is lowest (about 17.83M).
  • East has the largest regional share (27.23%) and West the smallest (22.49%).

Dataset and preparation

500 orders from 1 January 2025 to 16 May 2026, with order, customer, city, region, product, quantity, price, sales, profit and salesperson fields. The data is loaded from an Excel workbook with Power Query, where headers are promoted and column types are set.

Notes

  • All KPIs use Power BI’s built-in aggregations. This version has no custom DAX measures.
  • The monthly trend groups by calendar month only, so January to May combine two years while June to December include 2025 only.
  • The data source was a local Excel file that is not included, so the data will not refresh without a new source.
